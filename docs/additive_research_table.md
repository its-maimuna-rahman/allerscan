# Food Additive Hypersensitivity Research Table

Version: 1.0.0  
Last Reviewed: 2026-10-27  
Evidence Framework: Journal of Food Allergy (2021), Allergo Journal International (2022), Skypala et al. (2015), Andreozzi et al. (2019), and official EU/Codex/FDA registers.

## Evidence Tiers
- **Tier 1 (Regulated or well documented)**: Established immune or bronchospastic reactions; legally highlighted or documented anaphylaxis cohorts.
- **Tier 2 (Case reports of allergic reactions)**: Published case reports of IgE-mediated anaphylaxis, urticaria, or angioedema with positive skin tests/challenges.
- **Tier 3 (Commonly blamed, unconfirmed in blinded challenges)**: Anecdotally blamed or historical complaints; blinded placebo-controlled food challenges (DBPCFC) show no consistent reproducible reaction.
